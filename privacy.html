WEBSITE DISCLOSURE

Privacy & transparency.

Effective 20 October 2026

What this website is

WasteMatch is an independent, concept-stage business-service proposal by Premangshu Mal. This website provides public information for prospective commercial partners and buyers. It is not an operational waste exchange, a regulated advisory service or an offer of securities.

Information you enter

The contact form is implemented in your browser. When you select “Open email draft”, your browser requests that your device's configured email app open a draft containing the fields you entered. The website itself does not send that email to a server or store the form inputs. You must review and send the message in your mail app. If you use “Copy inquiry text”, the composed content is copied to your clipboard if you grant the relevant browser permission.

If you choose to send an email

Your email and any information you include will be received by the concept creator and your respective email providers, subject to their own practices. Please do not send confidential technical documents, regulated material information, personal identification records or trade secrets in an initial unsolicited inquiry.

Analytics, cookies and third parties

This website contains no intentional third-party analytics scripts, marketing pixels or site cookies. Hosting providers may process technical access and security logs independently. Outbound resource links open third-party websites with their own policies.

Accuracy and intellectual property

All exchange examples and calculator figures are hypothetical. WasteMatch makes no assertion of proprietary ownership of the general industrial symbiosis principle, registered trademark rights, patent rights or proven financial returns. Any eventual asset transfer or license must clearly define the applicable rights and deliverables.
