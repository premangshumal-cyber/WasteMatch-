01 / THE OPPORTUNITY

The material has value.
The connection is missing.

Many businesses approach surplus as a disposal issue. Others search for affordable inputs. WasteMatch explores the commercial opportunity between those two decisions.

THE BASIC FORMULA

Annual combined value = volume × (avoided disposal + conventional input cost − transport − processing).

Material sale price transfers value between parties; it does not create additional combined value.

This calculator does not assess technical suitability, demand, legal compliance, substitution ratio or actual market price. It is not a quote, valuation or forecast.
